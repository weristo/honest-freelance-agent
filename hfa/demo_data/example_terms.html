Example Freelance Marketplace: Terms and Fees

This page is fictional. It was written for the Honest Freelance Agent demo and describes no real company.

1. Fees

For fixed price projects the freelancer service fee is 8% of the project amount.

Fees are deducted when the client releases the payment.

2. Invoicing

Freelancers may invoice clients for completed milestones through a supported payment provider.

Each invoice must describe the delivered work and the agreed amount.

3. Prohibited conduct

You may not use automated software to submit bids or proposals on behalf of other users.

You may not create more than one account to collect additional sign-up rewards.

4. Payouts

Balances can be withdrawn once a week to a verified payout method.

Withdrawals are processed within three business days after the request is approved.

5. Disputes

If a client disputes a milestone, both parties are invited to share the delivered files and the written agreement.

A reviewer reads both sides and decides within ten business days.

6. Changes

We announce changes to these terms by e-mail at least thirty days before they take effect.

Continued use of the marketplace after that date means that you accept the updated terms.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through the help centre form on the marketplace website.

The help centre answers most questions within two business days, in English.

8. Glossary

A milestone is a part of a project with its own deliverable and its own payment.

A payout method is a bank account or wallet that the freelancer has verified in the account settings.

A client is the user who posts a project and pays for the work that the freelancer delivers.

9. Accessibility

The marketplace website supports keyboard navigation and screen readers on every page.

Clients and freelancers can switch the interface to a high contrast theme in the account settings.

10. Records

Invoices and payment receipts stay available in the account history for seven years.

Freelancers can export their invoice history as a spreadsheet at any time.
